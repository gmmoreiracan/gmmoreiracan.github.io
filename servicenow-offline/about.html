READING THE FIELDS

How the pages preserve evidence

Empty values stay visible. A field rendered as “(empty)” was blank on the captured record.

Historical snapshot. Dates and statuses reflect the captured instance at the time shown; they do not update with the live system.

Use and purpose. The assistant asset includes its intended outcome, human involvement, autonomy level, and data categories. The linked prompt and input/output records add context; the captured view has no separate use-case record linked to the asset.

Credential values: secret values are omitted. This local copy includes only the credential name and non-secret metadata.
