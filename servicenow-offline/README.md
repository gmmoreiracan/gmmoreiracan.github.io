# Inside Goodday: the system behind the story

Open index.html to browse the local library, or open any page in records/ directly. The pages are plain HTML with local CSS and JavaScript. They work offline and do not require a ServiceNow login.

## What is included

- A Technology Reference Model product record and a Technology Lifecycle Management risk record from Enterprise Architecture Workspace.
- Digital Integration DINTG0001007, its Digital Interface record, credential metadata, and information-object links.
- The Goodday Chore Assistant artificial intelligence asset, linked prompt, governance state, and related information-object context.
- Express List alert Alert0010033 after widening the time range to Last week, with impacted services, Service Dashboard health details, and the Goodday Household Web service map.
- DevOps change CHG0030012 with its Test Summaries and Commits related lists, plus a Pipeline Execution and its Step Executions.
- Prominent workspace galleries on each record page, including a full Artificial Intelligence Control Tower walkthrough of the Chore Assistant, its model, prompt, information objects, and business application.

## Snapshot notes

The record pages preserve what was visible in the authenticated development instance on 21 Oct 2026. Values that were blank in the source are rendered as “(empty)”. Secret values are omitted; the offline page includes only non-secret credential metadata.

The artificial intelligence system has a deployed asset state, governance lifecycle fields, use and purpose details, and related lists for its model, prompt, inputs and outputs, and business application. The instance did not expose a separate use-case record linked from this asset during capture; the Details tab's Use and Purpose section is reproduced as a section of the asset page.

This is a local read-only snapshot, not a live integration. Private instance links are omitted so the guide can be shared with readers who do not have access. The HTML does not copy platform application code.
